A battery might help, even without a rebate

Midday solar now earns less when it goes back to the grid on many SDG&E plans. A battery stores that power for the evening, when electricity costs the most, and can keep your lights on in an outage. Tesla, Enphase, FranklinWH and other options.

SDCP battery rebates: funding closing soon

About 6–8 weeks of funding left, first come, first served. Eligible customers may get roughly $2,000–$6,000 per battery depending on the type of project and battery. Funds and approval aren't guaranteed. Program details
