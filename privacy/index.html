Privacy Policy

yourenergybest.com · Effective November 22, 2026

This policy covers yourenergybest.com, run by Your Energy Best. The lumaria app has its own privacy policy.

What we collect

What you give us: your name, email, phone, address or ZIP, details about your home, energy use, solar system and interests, any bill you upload, appointment details, and what you send if you apply to work with us.

How you found us: the site that referred you and any campaign tags in the link.

Technical details: your IP address, browser and request times, used to run and protect the site.

Payments: Stripe takes your card details on its own checkout. We receive confirmation and your contact details, not your full card number.

How we use it

To answer you, prepare quotes and audits, book and confirm appointments, take payments, keep customer records, prevent spam and fraud, and improve the site. We may email you about your request and send newsletters or offers. You can unsubscribe from those at any time.

Who else handles it

Companies that run parts of our service: Zoho (customer records and email), Hostinger (hosting and email), Calendly (booking), Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (security checks and file delivery), and the providers behind the lumaria forms we link to, described in lumaria’s policy.

Licensed installers, contractors, financing companies, utilities and program administrators, when their part is needed for the quote, service or program you asked about.

Content providers your browser loads directly, such as Google Fonts, OpenStreetMap, Instagram, YouTube, Vimeo and code libraries. They receive your IP address and browser details under their own policies.

We may also share information when the law requires it or as part of a sale or transfer of the business.

We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

We send recurring or promotional texts only with your consent. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop or HELP for help. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for their marketing.

Browser storage and tracking signals

The site uses browser storage to remember form progress and how you found us, and a cookie for paid or signed-in areas. Because there is no common standard for Do Not Track signals, the site does not respond to them. Third-party content may collect information about your browsing under those companies’ policies.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to serve you, keep warranty, contract, tax and legal records, and resolve disputes. You can ask us to delete it sooner, and we will unless we need to keep it for one of those reasons.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop hearing from us. Email or call us below. We may need to confirm your identity, and we won’t treat you differently for asking.

Security and children

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure. This site is for adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We will post changes on this page with a new effective date.
